# Business Sales Dashboard

## Overview
This Power BI report provides a comprehensive analysis 
of business sales data across four key areas: sales 
performance, customer behaviour, product analysis, 
and KPI tracking.

## Key Insights
- Consumer segment contributes 51% of total sales
- Q4 records the highest product sales growth
- California leads all states in customer volume
- Business exceeds order targets with 626 vs target of 500
- Phones and Chairs are the top performing sub-categories

## Report Pages
- **Sales Overview** — segment, regional and quarterly trends
- **Customer Insights** — state level customer and ship mode analysis
- **Product Analysis** — cost, quantity and sales by sub-category
- **KPI Dashboard** — performance against targets for Orders, 
  Sales and Units Sold by Region and Year

## Tools & Skills Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX 
- Power Query (Merge, Append, Data Cleaning)
- Data Modelling
- KPI and Gauge Visuals
